Return & Refund Policy

What can be returned, what cannot, and how refunds are handled.

Cancellation before dispatch

An order may be cancelled at any time before it is dispatched, for a full refund of anything paid. Contact Everlume with the order number as early as possible; once a shipment has been handed to the carrier it can no longer be cancelled.

Materials that cannot be returned

For integrity and safety reasons, research materials that have shipped cannot be returned once the outer packaging or any seal has been opened. Everlume cannot verify the storage conditions of materials that have left its control, and will not return such materials to inventory or resell them.

Damaged, incorrect, or defective shipments

If a shipment arrives damaged, incomplete, or does not match what was ordered, contact Everlume within five business days of delivery with the order number and photographs of the packaging and contents. Everlume will replace the affected items or issue a refund, at the customer’s election, and will cover return shipping where a return is required.

Refund method and timing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Once Everlume issues a refund, the time to appear on a statement depends on the payment provider and the customer’s financial institution.

Orders declined at review

If Everlume declines an order during review — for eligibility, destination, or documentation reasons — nothing is charged. Where a payment was already taken, it is refunded in full.

How to start a return

Email Everlume with the order number and the reason for the request. Do not ship anything back before receiving written return authorization; unauthorized returns cannot be processed or refunded.
