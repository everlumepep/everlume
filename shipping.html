Shipping Policy

How Everlume prepares, ships, and confirms delivery of laboratory research materials.

Order review before shipment

Every order is reviewed before fulfillment. Everlume confirms the requested materials, quantities, destination eligibility, and the research-use acknowledgements submitted at checkout. Orders that cannot be confirmed are not shipped and are not charged.

Processing time

Confirmed orders are typically prepared within two to five business days. Everlume notifies the ordering contact by email if preparation will take longer, and orders are not dispatched until availability is verified.

Packaging

Materials ship in protective packaging appropriate to the format. Packaging is plain and does not display product names on the exterior. Storage and handling information accompanies the shipment where applicable.

Destinations and restrictions

Everlume ships to research entities at commercial or institutional addresses. Some destinations, materials, or quantities may be restricted by carrier policy or by applicable law; where a restriction applies, Everlume declines the order and notifies the requester rather than substituting or partially shipping without consent.

Shipping charges

Shipping is quoted after order review, based on destination, weight, and any handling requirements. The quoted amount is confirmed with the customer before any payment is requested. No shipping charge is applied at the time an order request is submitted.

Tracking and delivery

Tracking information is provided by email once a shipment is dispatched. Title and risk of loss pass on delivery to the carrier. Report visible damage or a discrepancy within five business days of delivery so Everlume can investigate with the carrier.

Undeliverable shipments

Shipments returned as undeliverable are inspected on receipt. Where the materials remain in acceptable condition, Everlume contacts the customer to arrange reshipment at the customer’s cost or to process a refund under the return policy.

Everlume presents materials for laboratory research and educational use only. Nothing on this site is medical advice, and no therapeutic, diagnostic, or efficacy claims are made. Questions: contact Everlume.
