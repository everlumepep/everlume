How to reach Everlume for documentation, availability, orders, and compliance questions.

Research and availability inquiries

For current availability, specifications, or documentation, submit the inquiry form on the catalog page. Include the materials and formats you are interested in and a brief description of the intended laboratory research.

Order questions

For a question about an existing order, include the order number in your message. Order status is also available in My Everlume once you are signed in.

Returns and shipment issues

Report a damaged, incomplete, or incorrect shipment within five business days of delivery. See the return policy for what is required and what can be returned.

Compliance and legal

Everlume presents materials for laboratory research and educational use only. Direct questions about research-use requirements, documentation, or destination restrictions through the inquiry form and mark them as a compliance question so they are routed appropriately.

What not to send

Do not send payment card numbers, bank details, government identifiers, or personal medical information through the inquiry form. Everlume does not request payment details by email and will never ask for them in a reply to an inquiry.

Response times

Inquiries are typically answered within two business days. Compliance and documentation requests may take longer where a review is required.

Everlume presents materials for laboratory research and educational use only. Nothing on this site is medical advice, and no therapeutic, diagnostic, or efficacy claims are made. Questions: contact Everlume.
